Questions? We've got answers

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, tap Checkout, fill in your name, phone and address, then send. Your order comes to us by text message or WhatsApp, and we'll reply to confirm it and share payment details.

How do I pay?

After we confirm your order, you can pay with Zelle, Venmo, Cash App or PayPal. We ship once payment arrives.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–2 business days and usually arrive 3–5 days later. We'll send you tracking once it ships.

Can I exchange a size?

Yes. Unused items with tags can be exchanged within 14 days. Just message us.

Are your products safe for newborns?

We choose BPA-free, non-toxic and skin-friendly items. Each product page tells you more about the materials.
