Questions

How much is shipping?

Shipping depends on your address and order size, so we work it out for each order. After you send your order, we'll text you the exact total, including shipping and any sales tax. You only pay once you've approved it.

How do I pay?

After you approve your total, you can pay with Zelle, Venmo, Cash App or PayPal. We ship as soon as payment arrives.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–2 business days and usually arrive 3–5 days later. We'll text you tracking once it's on its way.

Can I exchange a size?

Yes. Unused items with tags can be exchanged or returned within 14 days of delivery. Just text us. See our return policy.

Are your products safe for newborns?

We choose BPA-free, non-toxic and skin-friendly items, and each product page describes its materials. Please also read our safety tips.

Need a hand choosing?

We're happy to help with sizes, gifts and anything else.
